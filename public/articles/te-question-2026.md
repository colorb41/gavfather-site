# Should I Stay or Should I Go: The TE Question

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

George Costanza's entire life is a series 
of decisions made too late, too early, 
or based entirely on the wrong information.

Every fantasy drafter does the same thing 
with tight end.

Do I take Kittle in round 3? Is that too early? 
Should I wait? What if someone else takes him? 
What if I wait and he is gone? What if I take 
him and he gets hurt? What if I do the opposite 
of what I normally do?

George tried that. It worked for exactly one episode.

The Gavfather System has spent 22 years 
analyzing tight end production. Here is 
what the data actually says.

---

## The TE Problem

The gap between TE1 and TE13 is the largest 
positional gap in fantasy football.

In 2025 Trey McBride averaged 12.63 PPG. 
The 13th best TE averaged 7.07 PPG. 
That is a 5.56 point per game advantage � 
every single week � from having an elite TE.

Over 17 weeks that is 94 points. 
Nearly six full wins in a typical scoring system.

No other position has this gap. 
The difference between WR1 and WR13 
is smaller. The difference between RB1 
and RB13 is smaller. TE is unique.

This is why the decision matters so much. 
And why everyone overthinks it like George 
trying to decide whether to shake his boss's hand.
