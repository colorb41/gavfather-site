# The WR Camp Board: Speed, Toes, and a 22-Spot Discount

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

Ted Lasso would call this a
belief problem. The market
believes last year's highlight.
Camp is running the 40 again.

The Gavfather WR board after
this week's notes is not a
new religion. Puka Nacua is
still overall 3. Ja'Marr Chase
is still overall 5. The edges
are where camp actually paid.

---

## The good kind of camp news

**Terry McLaurin, WAS.**
HEALTHY. Clocking 22 mph in camp.
No issues. Daniels connection
is real. The model has him WR22,
which is exactly where the market
has him — so this is not a BUY
on price. It is a confirmation.
If you were fading McLaurin
because Washington "always
disappoints," camp just told
you to stop.

**Malik Nabers, NYG.**
RETURNING. ACL + meniscus,
ahead of schedule, running
routes. Worst case: misses
1–2 games. The camp engine
still applies 0.93x. The ADP
market has him WR16. We have
him WR25 — a FADE on price,
not on talent. Pay for the
player. Do not pay for the
"he's back early" tax.

**Rashee Rice, KC.**
Healthy and practicing.
That is the sentence people
wanted. It is not enough.
Suspension risk remains.
Mahomes is cleared for camp
and taking 1st-team reps —
do not stack a Rice fade on
a quarterback who is practicing.
We have Rice WR28. The market
has him WR6. That is a 22-spot
FADE on Rice's price, not on
whether Mahomes exists in September.

---

## The bad kind of camp news

**Ricky Pearsall, SF.**
Season-ending PCL. 0.00 PPG.
Market still has him WR53.
If he is on your board, delete
the row.

**Emeka Egbuka, TB.**
Left practice Wednesday with
what Yahoo is calling a minor
toe issue. We had him as a BUY
(WR12 vs ADP WR17) at 12.50 PPG.
Camp applied 0.93x. Week 1 still
EXPECTED OK. Toe injuries on
receivers are not "he's fine"
until he practices again. Keep
him in the WR2 conversation.
Do not climb for him on a rumor
that it is minor.

**Jaylen Waddle, MIA.**
Muscle tightness, left practice
August 5. MONITOR. Has injury
history. We have him WR30 vs
ADP WR27 — a small FADE. Miami
with McDaniel still looks
explosive on paper. Waddle
has to stay on the field for
that paper to cash.

**Alec Pierce, IND.**
PUP. Ankle. Expected off the
list "sometime in the next
week or so." We have him WR48.
The market has him WR25. That
is a 23-spot FADE. "Coming off
PUP soon" is not a draft strategy.

---

## The discounts camp created

These are the BUY receivers
after ADP and camp are both
in the file:

**Jauan Jennings, SF.**
WR46 vs ADP WR68. +22 spots.
Pearsall is gone. This is
the vacancy pick, not a
sleeper you invented.

**Brian Thomas Jr., JAX.**
WR16 vs ADP WR35. +19 spots.
The model liked him before
camp. Camp did not give you
a reason to stop.

**Stefon Diggs, NE.**
WR26 vs ADP WR45. +19 spots.
New England's passing game
is not a mystery to the
model. It is a mystery to
people who still think Diggs
is a 2023 story.

**Romeo Doubs, GB.**
WR33 vs ADP WR52. +19 spots.
Kraft is back. The Packers
passing game is a real
environment again.

**Deebo Samuel Sr., WAS.**
WR27 vs ADP WR42. +15 spots.
McLaurin is flying. Deebo
is the other Washington
receiver the market is
still treating like a
scheme orphan.

**Kayshon Boutte, NE.**
WR55 vs ADP WR78. +23 spots.
That is a late-round BUY
if your league is deep
enough to care. If it is
not, take Diggs instead
and go home.
