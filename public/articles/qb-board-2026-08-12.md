# The QB Board After Camp: Confidence Intervals, Not Vibes

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

Moneyball was never about finding
a better scouting adjective.
It was about shrinking the
error bars until the price
looked stupid.

That is the quarterback board
on August 12.

Josh Allen is QB1, overall 6,
26.38 PPG. The market agrees.
There is no edge there. There
is a floor. Take it if he falls.
Do not talk yourself into a
"value" that is just the
consensus pick with extra steps.

The edge is everyone else.

---

## The top of the board (high confidence)

**Josh Allen, BUF.** QB1. Neutral vs ADP.
The model and the market shook hands.
Move on.

**Jalen Hurts, PHI.** QB2 on our board.
ADP QB8. Six-spot BUY. 22.41 PPG.
Philadelphia's rushing QB profile
does not need a camp surprise to
be real. The market is still
drafting him like a mid-tier
QB1. We are not.

**Lamar Jackson, BAL.** QB3. ADP QB3.
Neutral. Same story as Allen:
the price is fair. The question
is whether you want to pay it
before the skill-position run.

**Drake Maye, NE.** QB4. ADP QB2.
Slightly behind the market, not
a fade worth a paragraph. New
England's offense is why Diggs
and Henderson are BUYs. Maye is
the engine. The engine is not
the discount.

**Dak Prescott, DAL.** QB5. ADP QB5.
Neutral. CeeDee is overall 14.
The Cowboys passing game is
priced in.

---

## The actual discounts

**Justin Herbert, LAC.**
QB6. ADP QB13. Seven-spot BUY.
19.50 PPG.

Mike McDaniel is the coordinator.
Herbert called the scheme an
oasis. The model applied the
scheme bump weeks ago. The
market still has him outside
the top 12. That gap has not
closed. Camp did not give
Chargers people a reason to
wait. If you need a QB1 who
is not Allen/Hurts/Lamar money,
this is the name.

**Baker Mayfield, TB.**
QB7. ADP QB17. BUY.
Egbuka's toe is the only new
wrinkle in Tampa. Baker's
price was already wrong.
A minor toe on his WR2 does
not make Baker a fade. It
makes the Bucs a monitor.

**Jared Goff, DET.**
QB9. ADP QB15. BUY.
Gibbs and Amon-Ra are both
top-10 overall. Goff is the
point guard in that offense
and the market is still
drafting him like a game
manager you settle for.

---

## The error bars that got wider this week

**Patrick Mahomes, KC.**
QB10. ADP QB12. 18.84 PPG
before the camp cut.

ACL + LCL, Week 15 2025.
Status: RECOVERING.
Week 1: UNCERTAIN. ESPN is
still asking if he starts.
The camp engine applies 0.88x
and says fade until confirmed.

A two-spot ADP "discount"
is not a discount when the
player might not play.
This is not the 2022 Mahomes
argument. This is a medical
file. Treat it like one.

**C.J. Stroud, HOU.**
QB22 on the live board.
Not expected to play in
Thursday's preseason opener
vs the Chargers.

Camp status: SEVERE.
PPG 15.71 → 10.21.
Week 1: UNLIKELY.

Sitting the preseason opener
can be protection. It can
also be the first public
admission that something
is wrong. Until Houston
gives a clearance, Stroud
is not a late-round dart.
He is a news alert.

**Tua Tagovailoa, ATL.**
Undisclosed camp issue.
Questionable. Atlanta has
Penix. The camp note is
explicit: backup risk with
Penix available. Tua is
not a streamer you stash
"just in case." He is a
room that can flip in a
week.

**Bo Nix, DEN.**
Will not play in the
preseason opener (August 12).
Camp tagged that NEUTRAL —
starters sit preseason games
all the time. Do not confuse
Nix sitting with Stroud
sitting. One is a plan.
One is a flag. The file
already made that distinction.

**Joe Burrow, CIN.**
QB17 on our board. ADP QB4.
Thirteen-spot FADE. Chase is
still overall 5. Burrow's
price assumes a full-volume
passing season the model
will not pay for at that ADP.
Camp did not fix the price.

## How to draft it

If you want the floor: Allen,
Hurts, Lamar.

If you want the edge: Herbert,
then Baker or Goff.

If you want a story: wait
for Mahomes to be confirmed
or leave him on the board.

If you want a cheap QB2:
do not use Stroud to get
there until Thursday's
inactive list is a memory
and not a pattern.
