# The Vacancy Report: Who Inherits the Work

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

Ocean's Eleven is a movie about a hole
in a vault and the people who walk through it.

Training camp is the same job.
Someone goes down. Someone else gets paid.

The Gavfather camp engine processed 1,400+
news items this week. Four vacancies actually
move a Labor Day draft. The rest is noise
the podcasts will still be talking about
in September.

Here is who inherits the work.

---

## The Rule

When a starter's Week 1 outlook is OUT
or UNLIKELY, the model does not "wait and see."
It cuts the projection and looks at who
is left on the depth chart with a BUY
signal against ADP.

Vacancy is not a vibe. It is leftover
targets, leftover carries, leftover
red-zone looks.

---

## San Francisco: Pearsall is a zero

Ricky Pearsall. PCL. Season-ending.
The board has him at 0.00 projected PPG.
The market is still drafting him as WR53.

That is not a fade. That is a player
who should not be on a roster.

The vacancy: Jauan Jennings.
We have him WR46. The market has him WR68.
Twenty-two spots of positional disagreement.
BUY.

Pearsall's targets do not vanish.
They go to Jennings, to the remaining
49ers skill group, and to whoever
is healthy enough to run the route.
Jennings is the one the model already
liked before the injury. The injury
just made the price more wrong.

George Kittle is still on the board
as TE3. He should not be. Achilles tear
from the January playoffs. PUP. Week 1
outlook: UNLIKELY, and San Francisco
opens in Australia. That is not a
"he'll be ready" profile. That is a
vacancy at tight end the market has
not fully priced.

McBride and Bowers stay TE1 and TE2.
Do not spend a TE3 pick hoping Kittle
is the exception to every Achilles
timeline you have ever watched.

---

## Houston: Stroud is not playing Thursday

C.J. Stroud is not expected to see
the field in the preseason opener
against the Chargers.

The camp engine treated that as SEVERE.
PPG 15.71 down to 10.21. Rank 35 down
toward the 150s. Week 1 outlook: UNLIKELY.

Sitting a preseason opener is not
automatically a season. It is a signal
that Houston is protecting something.
Until that something has a name and
a clearance date, Stroud is not a
QB2 you "get cheap." He is a roster
spot with a medical question attached.

---

## Kansas City: the vacancy is still Mahomes

Patrick Mahomes. ACL + LCL, Week 15 2025.
Status: RECOVERING. Week 1: UNCERTAIN.
ESPN is still asking if he is the
Week 1 starter. That is the tell.

The board has him QB10, overall 69,
18.84 PPG before the camp cut.
The camp engine applies 0.88x and
tells you to fade until he is confirmed.

Rashee Rice is healthy and practicing.
That is the good news. The bad news
is the same as last month: suspension
risk plus a quarterback who might not
be out there. Rice is a FADE at WR6
ADP when we have him WR28.

If Mahomes is not Week 1, the vacancy
is the entire Kansas City passing game.
Do not build a team that needs that
building to be open on Labor Day.

---

## Green Bay: the vacancy that closed

Tucker Kraft came off PUP.
ACL last season. Status: RETURNING.
Multiplier 1.05. Ahead of schedule.

This is the other kind of vacancy
report. The job was open. It is
closing. Kraft is a rising TE the
model already had in the TE15 range.
Camp says the Packers passing game
benefits. Believe the practice reports
more than the "he just came off PUP"
scare quotes.
