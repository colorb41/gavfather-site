## What Is the Boom-Consistency Index

The Boom-Consistency Index is simple.

Boom rate: the percentage of games where 
a player scored 20 or more fantasy points.

Consistency score: the inverse of week-to-week 
variance. How reliably does this player 
show up every week.

The index multiplies them together.

High boom rate AND high consistency = elite.
High boom rate BUT low consistency = lottery ticket.
Low boom rate AND high consistency = safe floor.
Low on both = avoid.

The market obsesses over boom rate alone. 
Fantasy Twitter loves the guy who scored 
38 points in Week 7. But boom rate without 
consistency is exactly what Ted warned about � 
talent without process. Flashy but unreliable.

The data says: you want both.

## Why This Works

The machine found this independently. 
We did not design it. We asked the model 
what predicts next year fantasy performance 
and this is what it found in 11,054 
player-seasons going back to 2004.

The reason it works is behavioral. 
Players who score big regularly AND 
show up every week are doing something 
fundamentally right � they have genuine 
roles, real opportunity, and the talent 
to convert that opportunity consistently.

Players who only boom occasionally are 
usually benefiting from situational 
touchdowns, garbage time inflation, or 
scheme-dependent plays that do not repeat.

The market prices the boom. 
The data rewards the process.
