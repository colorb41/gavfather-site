# Tampa Bay Completed Zero Passes and Won

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

On Sunday the Tampa Bay Buccaneers played a professional football game against the Atlanta Falcons, and Baker Mayfield attempted **three** forward passes.

He completed **zero** of them.

Tampa Bay won **16-0**.

I want to explain how this works to readers who are not football fans, and also to readers who are, because nobody understands it. In the rain at Raymond James Stadium, the Buccaneers handed the ball to Bucky Irving **27** times for **170** yards and to Kenny Gainwell **25** times for **140** yards, and they rushed for **324** yards, a franchise record. They finished with **minus-2** net passing yards, because Mayfield also got sacked. ESPN calculated that Tampa got **100.6%** of its offense on the ground (324 rushing yards out of 322 total), which is the kind of percentage you normally only see on a supplement label.

According to ESPN, the last team to go an entire game without a completion was the **1974 Buffalo Bills**. They beat the Jets 16-12. Before Sunday, no two teammates in NFL history had each run it **25** times for **125** yards in the same game. Mayfield, per ESPN, "threw more downfield blocks than he had completions," which is true, and which is also the most Baker Mayfield sentence ever written.

Asked whether he'd ever won without a completion, coach Todd Bowles said, "I have not, but there is a first time for everything."

(The *only* reason this didn't count as a moral victory for Atlanta's defense is that Michael Penix Jr. went **8 for 24** with **four interceptions**. The Falcons, you will notice, *did* complete passes. They just completed some of them to Tampa Bay.)

I am not making this up.

## The call on the Tampa backfield

Here is the part where your league-mate spends 60% of his waiver budget on Kenny Gainwell. Do not be that league-mate.

Going into Sunday, our weekly board had Gainwell at **RB49** with an official **low-usage flag**: **6.1** touches a game, **35%** of the snaps. Then he got 25 carries. The model is not embarrassed by this. The model has seen weather before. On the rest-of-season board he's **RB30** (**10.2** points a game), a touch above his preseason consensus of **RB35**, and that's about right: a very good handcuff who has a lead-back day whenever it rains sideways.

**Bucky Irving** is the guy. He came into Week 8 at **20.8** touches a game, and he's **RB26** on our ROS board versus **RB21** consensus ADP. Tampa plays **Sunday night in Chicago**. Start Irving. Stash Gainwell if he's free, but don't bid on him like he's the starter.

**Mayfield:** in superflex, keep starting him. He's **QB12** on our ROS board (**19.0** a game) versus **QB17** in ADP. One monsoon is not a trend. (Ted Hurst dropped what would have been a 28-yard touchdown on Mayfield's very first throw, so technically Baker was one wet pair of hands away from a 1974-proof afternoon.)

## Baltimore: one play

Lamar Jackson missed three games with a high ankle sprain. On Sunday in Buffalo he came back.

On the Ravens' **first offensive play**, his right hand, the throwing one, hit Bills pass rusher Greg Rousseau as he threw. He went straight to the locker room. He came back to the sideline in the third quarter **wearing a cast**. Coach Jesse Minter says Jackson will have **thumb surgery** and go on **injured reserve**, which means at least **four** games, and when asked if it could be season-ending, Minter said, "potentially."

Then backup **Tyler Huntley** hurt his toe in the second quarter. Minter says Huntley "probably" won't play Thursday.

That left **Joe Fagnano**, an undrafted rookie making his NFL debut, who went **10 for 21** for **91** yards with an interception and a lost fumble, and who also got hurt, on a low hit, and finished the game anyway. Buffalo won **36-10**. Zay Flowers was held without a catch for the **first time in his career**.

On Monday the team's own website, which I want to stress is run *by the Ravens*, reported that the Ravens "don't have any fully healthy quarterbacks currently on the 53-man roster." So the Ravens signed **Austin Reed** to the practice squad. Reed went 6 for 12 for 59 yards in the preseason, and earlier he led the UFL in passing. Also the kicker hurt his groin, so a different kicker is kicking. The Jaguars come to Baltimore on **Thursday night**. The Ravens are holding one practice.

A personal disclosure: in October I spent **$36** of waiver money on Tyler Huntley *specifically as Lamar insurance*. On Sunday the house burned down, and in the same game the insurance policy got turf toe.

**The calls:**

- **Lamar Jackson:** IR slot, today. In fairness to the model, our ROS board was last refreshed Sunday at **12:15 p.m. ET**, before he took a snap, and it still has him at **QB3** (**21.8** a game). That's the healthy number. He is not healthy. Do not let anyone trade you their "QB3" this week for a running back.
- **Fagnano** is **QB95** on the board at **4.35** projected points. In superflex, if he's your best option, your problem is not Joe Fagnano.
- **Zay Flowers:** our Week 8 board had him **WR3**, on a **31%** target share, and he finished with zero catches. The target share is real. The quarterback is not. He's **WR18** ROS (preseason ADP **WR13**). Keep him, but he's a flex this week, not a WR1, on a Thursday, with whoever is upright.
