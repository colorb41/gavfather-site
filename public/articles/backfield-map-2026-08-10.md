# The Backfield Map: Who Is Actually Locked In

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

The Bear is a show about a kitchen
where everyone thinks they are the
lead and only one station is actually
firing.

Running back rooms in August are
the same kitchen.

The Gavfather board still has
Jonathan Taylor at overall 1
(20.17 PPG) and Bijan Robinson
at overall 2 (19.36 PPG, camp-adjusted
up to 19.75 after the extension).
That is not a hot take. That is
two backfields that told you,
in writing, who the starter is.

Here is the rest of the map
after this week's camp file.

---

## Locked in

**Bijan Robinson, ATL.**
Extension signed. Team commitment
confirmed. Camp status: HEALTHY.
The model already had him RB2.
The contract just removed the
last excuse to talk yourself
into a committee.

**Jonathan Taylor, IND.**
Overall 1. 20.17 PPG. No camp flag.
If your league mates are still
waiting for "the other shoe,"
they can wait while you take him.

**Jahmyr Gibbs, DET.**
Overall 4. 18.60 PPG. Listed
questionable in the scrape this
week. The camp engine applied a
mild 0.93x. That is a monitor,
not a fade. Detroit's offense
does not replace Gibbs with a
committee you want to draft.

---

## The prices that are still wrong

**Tyrone Tracy Jr., NYG.**
Biggest BUY on the entire board.
We have him RB29. The market has
him RB54. Twenty-five spots.

Nabers is returning from ACL +
meniscus, running routes, ahead
of schedule. Worst case he misses
a game or two. When Nabers is
on the field, New York's offense
is a different animal. Tracy is
the back in that offense and the
market is still drafting him like
a handcuff.

**TreVeyon Henderson, NE.**
RB10 on our board. RB24 at ADP.
Fourteen-spot BUY. New England
is not hiding the touches. The
market is.

**J.K. Dobbins, DEN.**
RB21 vs ADP RB32. BUY. Also
listed questionable. Take the
discount, do not ignore the tag.
Denver with a healthy Dobbins
is a different backfield than
Denver without one.

**James Conner, ARI.**
RB30 vs ADP RB48. BUY. Also
questionable this week. Same
rule as Dobbins: the price is
right if he is practicing. The
price is a trap if you need him
to be a lock on August 10.

---

## The rooms that are not locked

**Zach Charbonnet, SEA.**
PUP. Surgery. Camp engine cut
him 7.85 to 6.44 PPG. The board
still has a mild BUY vs ADP
because the market never loved
him either. Do not spend a pick
on a PUP back because the ADP
gap looks pretty.

**Christian McCaffrey, SF.**
Overall 7. RB4. Questionable in
the weekly scrape. Mild 0.93x.
CMC at this ADP is not a bargain
and not a fade. He is a first-round
bet that the 49ers usage stays
historic. Camp has not killed
that bet. It also has not made
it safer.

**Rachaad White, WAS
RB25 vs ADP RB37. BUY. Tampa's
backfield is still a committee
with a toe issue on Egbuka
stealing the headlines. White
is the process pick if you
need an RB2 who will get
volume even when the box score
looks messy.

---

## How to use this on draft night

Take the locked-in backs early
if they fall. Do not "get cute"
on Bijan or Taylor because
everyone else is scared of
the round.

Use the BUY backs — Tracy,
Henderson, Dobbins, Conner —
in the middle rounds where
the market is still asleep.

Leave the PUP and "hopeful"
rooms on the board. August
vacancies at running back
almost never get filled by
the guy you drafted as a
lottery ticket. They get
filled by the guy who was
already getting the carries.

Full rankings at thegavfather.com/rankings.
Updated as camp notes land.

An offer your roster can't refuse.
