# The Back Who Could Practice Stopped Practicing

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

Last Tuesday I told you to take TreVeyon Henderson because he could practice.

He has not practiced since August 24.

I am not making this up. That is the official Week 1 injury report for tomorrow's Kickoff Game, which is on a Wednesday, which is still illegal, which we already yelled about, and which is somehow still happening.

The National Football League — a large American organization that schedules football the way a toddler schedules naptime — opens the season tomorrow night with a Super Bowl rematch (Patriots at Seahawks, 8:20 p.m. ET). Then, on Thursday, it asks the 49ers and Rams to play in Melbourne, Australia, on Netflix, at 8:35 p.m. ET, which is Friday morning for the humans who live there. Sunday is when the cowards play.

In between those facts, the Offer I sold you last week got an ankle.

---

## An anthropology of "did not participate"

On the Patriots' Week 1 report, TreVeyon Henderson is listed as Did Not Participate. Reporting around that line says he is unlikely for tomorrow. If you drafted him off last week's column as your RB2: you own a second-year back who is currently a spreadsheet entry, and a veteran named Rhamondre Stevenson who is currently a person.

Our Sept. 7 Half-PPR file has Henderson at overall 70. Stevenson is overall 73. That is not "committee." That is two names sitting next to each other while one of them sits out practice. Monitor the final inactive list before you start either of them like a hero.

I am not walking back the math that put Henderson ahead of consensus when he was practicing. I am walking back the part where I said "take the back who can practice" and then the back stopped practicing. The data updates. So do we.
