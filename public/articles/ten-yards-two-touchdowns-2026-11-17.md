# Ten Carries, Ten Yards, Two Touchdowns

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

Two weeks ago I wrote that the Baltimore Ravens had no fully healthy quarterbacks on the roster. I want to be clear that I meant this as an observation, not a suggestion.

The Ravens took it as a suggestion. Last week they traded with the Arizona Cardinals for **Jacoby Brissett**, and on Monday night he made his first start for Baltimore against the Los Angeles Chargers, who came in **0-8**.

I need to explain the stakes to readers who are not football fans. Entering the game, the Chargers and Ravens had lost a combined **15** straight games, which according to the Associated Press is a record for a Monday night game. ESPN put this on national television on purpose. (Somewhere a network executive looked at this matchup and said, "Yes. Monday. Prime time." That person still has a job, which should give hope to all of us.)

Brissett did his part. He went **33 of 49 for 340 yards**. Then, with **2:03** left and Baltimore driving, Zay Flowers got stripped, the Chargers recovered, and the Ravens' final Hail Mary fell incomplete. Los Angeles won **23-20** for its first victory since Week 16 of *last season*.

And now we arrive at Derrick Henry, who carried the ball **10** times for **10** yards.

He also scored **two touchdowns**.

This is the part of fantasy football that anthropologists will one day study, the way they study cargo cults. In real football, the Ravens ran **15** times for **16** yards, got outrushed **160-16**, and lost to a winless team. In fantasy football, Henry had a perfectly respectable night, because our sport awards six points for standing near the goal line when somebody hands you the ball. Somewhere a man who started Henry is telling his wife it was "a good game for us." It was not a good game for anybody in Baltimore, including the parking attendants.

I am not making this up.

## Meanwhile, elsewhere in civilization

- The Minnesota Vikings traded with the Raiders for **Kirk Cousins**, whom they had previously let leave, and started him **five days** later. He went **21 of 31 for 207 yards**, two touchdowns and a pick, and the Vikings beat Green Bay **30-10** while sacking Jordan Love **eight** times. This is the NFL equivalent of texting your ex at 2 a.m., except it worked.
- In Munich, Detroit's **Aidan Hutchinson** suffered a significant knee injury **while celebrating a sack.** I want to stress that the sack itself went fine.
- The Falcons recovered an onside kick against Kansas City. According to CBS, it was the first successful onside kick of the season, after **23** attempts. The Falcons lost **35-13**, so the onside kick is the only thing in this paragraph that worked.
- The Cleveland Browns came back from **17-0** down to beat Houston, and are now the first team in NFL history whose first six wins all came by **four points or fewer**. The Browns are not a football team so much as a cardiologist's retirement plan.
- The 49ers, who play Minnesota on Sunday in **Mexico City**, are spending this week practicing in **Colorado Springs** to get used to the altitude. Mike Evans (Achilles tendinitis) flew in to join them and is listed as questionable. So to recap: to prepare for a home game in another country, San Francisco went to Colorado. The players will be fine. I am more worried about whoever does their expense reports.

## The calls

*All board numbers below are from our live rest-of-season Half PPR board as of Sunday's data update, which ran before Monday night. ADP is consensus positional ADP.*

**HOLD Derrick Henry.** The board still has him **RB4** against an **RB6** ADP. Before Monday he was averaging **19.3** touches a game on **54%** of the snaps, and the goal-line role clearly still belongs to him. Monday was ugly, the offensive line was worse, and someone in your league is going to offer you a WR3 and a "trust me." Do not trust them. If your league-mate sells the 10-yard night, you buy it.

**START Omarion Hampton, but don't trade the farm.** He went **22 carries for 105 yards** and a touchdown, plus Justin Herbert ran in another one. But coming into the week, Hampton was averaging **12.7** touches on **42%** of the snaps, and the board has him **RB14** against an **RB12** ADP, which is about right. One big game is a game. Two in a row is a role. Start him this week and check back.

**BUY Jacoby Brissett in superflex.** Board **QB23**, ADP **QB26**. He now has a starting job, Mark Andrews (**8** catches, **82** yards on Monday) and Flowers, and a running game that needs his help in the way that a sinking boat needs a bucket. As a QB2 in superflex, he will throw it 40-plus times because the Ravens have no other choice.

**BUY Justin Jefferson, ignore Kirk Cousins.** Jefferson caught **9** balls for **74** yards in Cousins' first game back. The board has him **WR5** against a **WR7** ADP, with a **31%** target share coming into the week. Cousins himself is **QB26** on our board with an "Avoid" tier. This is the rare trade that makes the Vikings much better at real football and changes nothing about your fantasy quarterbacks. Celebrate on Jefferson's behalf.

**HOLD Deebo; Keenan Allen is a bench piece.** With Evans possibly back in Mexico City, the 49ers WR room is about to get crowded. Our board has Deebo Samuel **WR33** vs **WR50** ADP, Keenan Allen **WR42** vs **WR59**, and Evans **WR30** vs **WR27**. Deebo is the hold. Allen is the guy who loses the most snaps once Evans returns.

**DON'T PANIC-BUY a Patriots fix.** Drake Maye left Munich after the opening drive with what's been reported as an ankle fracture, was still in Germany for testing on Monday, and Mike Vrabel said he doesn't expect him back out of the Week 11 bye. If you need a replacement QB, use your waiver budget on a guy with a job, like Brissett, rather than a rookie quarterback the board has as **QB40** (Fernando Mendoza).

## The Offer

Here's the one the market hasn't caught up to.

**BUY Jonah Coleman (DEN).** Board **RB24**. Consensus ADP **RB58**. That's a **34-spot** gap, and the usage backs it up. Since he came back from IR, his carries have gone **9 → 11 → 17**, and on Nov. 8 he got his first NFL start over J.K. Dobbins (**17** carries for **57** yards plus **4** catches, while Dobbins got **4** carries). Denver had its bye in Week 10. The catch: RJ Harvey is expected back from IR as soon as Week 12, and Sean Payton would rather go to the dentist than name a starting running back. That's why he's an RB2/flex and not an RB1. But at RB58 prices, you're paying for a backup and getting the guy who starts.

(For the record, the board also still has Dobbins at **RB23**. I'm counting that as the model being polite to a veteran. Don't buy Dobbins.)

**Deep BUY: Caleb Douglas (MIA).** Board **WR35** against a **WR81** ADP. On Sunday he played **84%** of the snaps and got **25%** of the targets (**8** looks, **3** catches, **71** yards), after a **6**-catch, **70**-yard game the week before. The quarterback is Kyle McCord and the Bills are next, so think WR4 with upside, not an every-week starter. Still, the market has him ranked like a guy who isn't playing, and he's playing nearly every snap.
