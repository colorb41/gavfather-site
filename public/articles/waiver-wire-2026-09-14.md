### Stats check

- **Volume > efficiency theater.** Gibbs, Jeanty, Henry, Skattebo, and Allen won on touches and opportunity, not one random YAC spike.
- **Snap / route share beats the box score.** Wicks out-snapped Lemon. Vele played 82 of 90. Black earned 43% snaps. Gesicki scored big on **21 of 64** snaps — that is the trap.
- **Offense environment still matters.** Miami can make Douglas the WR1 and still starve the position. Carolina can make Coker a must-add because Young is pushing the ball. Do not treat every Week 1 WR boom the same.

## FAAB logic (read this first)

We do not know your budget. Bid **percent of remaining FAAB**.

| Tier | % of budget | When to use it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panic / season-shaping | **18–28%** | Clear RB2 / every-week FLEX with roster scarcity, or the best available replacement for an IR star |
| Strong add | **10–17%** | Real role (snaps + routes) with multi-week runway |
| Standard add | **5–9%** | Useful FLEX / cuff / injury fill |
| Speculative | **1–4%** | Look-ahead stashes others will ignore |
| $0 / leave | **0%** | Box-score mirages and temporary target spikes |

**Rules we are using this week:**

1. **Role permanence first.** Snaps, routes, and injury-driven opportunity beat raw fantasy points.
2. **Do not pay WR1 prices for run-first offenses** (Miami, Baltimore depth).
3. **Handcuffs with standalone path** beat pure lottery tickets when RB depth is thin.
4. **Save powder for MNF surprises.** If you bid everything tonight, you cannot react to Arrowhead.

## Waiver priority (our order)

Players already rostered in most 10-team leagues (Coker ~60%+, Wan'Dale, etc.) are noted. Priority is for typical 12-team half-PPR.

### 1. Kaelon Black (RB, SF) — Priority 1
**FAAB: 18–25%** (need RB: push 25–28%)

**Why he is on the wire legitimately:** out-carried McCaffrey, 43% snaps, clear RB2 over Jordan James. Even if Australia / cramps inflated the split, he is the only SF handcuff that matters — and SF is favored the next two weeks (MIA, ARI), which is the exact script for a Corum-style FLEX.

**Why not higher than 28%:** if CMC reclaims 20+ touches next week, Black is a cuff, not a weekly starter. Do not mortgage the season.

### 2. Devaughn Vele (WR, NO) — Priority 2
**FAAB: 12–18%**

**Basis:** 82 of 90 snaps, on the field with Olave in 2-WR, Jordyn Tyson on IR/hamstring. The 90-play, 56-pass game will not repeat. The **role** can. That is enough for every-week FLEX in PPR/half-PPR until Tyson returns.

### 3. Romeo Doubs (WR, NE) — Priority 3 (if available)
**FAAB: 10–16%**

**Basis:** A.J. Brown is on IR (high ankle, four-game minimum). Doubs is the $68M veteran who should absorb boundary work even after a zero-catch debut. If he is already rostered (~55%), skip to DeMario Douglas.

### 4. Deebo Samuel Sr. (WR, SF) — Priority 3b (where still free)
**FAAB: 10–15%**

**Basis:** Stribling is out ~a month. Deebo already produced and fits Shanahan’s CMC-preservation plan as a runner/receiver. We had him as a BUY at WR33 — if he is somehow still free, that is a gift.

### 5. Dontayvion Wicks (WR, PHI) — Priority 4
**FAAB: 6–10%**

**Basis:** nearly mirrored DeVonta Smith in snaps/routes and beat Makai Lemon. The box score (2–73–1) looks splashy; the **route share** is the real reason. Soft upcoming slate (TEN, CHI).

### 6. Caleb Douglas (WR, MIA) — Priority 5
**FAAB: 5–9%** (do **not** go double digits)

**Basis:** real alpha role (86% routes, 26% targets, 5–94). Cap the bid because Miami is run-first and Week 2 is at San Francisco. You are buying the role, not a WR2 projection.

### 7. DeMario Douglas (WR, NE) — Priority 6 / look-ahead
**FAAB: 4–8%**

**Basis:** led NE in targets (7) even before Brown’s exit; ~1% owned in many leagues. While everyone fights over Doubs, Douglas is the PPR floor in McDaniels’ committee. Four weeks of Brown IR is a long runway.

### 8. Braelon Allen (RB, NYJ) — Priority 7
**FAAB: 3–7%**

**Basis:** 40% snaps and 10 carries behind Hall. Not a standalone starter. Best pure cuff on the board after Black if your RB room is thin.

### 9. Adonai Mitchell (WR, NYJ) — Priority 8 / look-ahead
**FAAB: 3–6%**

**Basis:** 69% snap share with Garrett Wilson; Omar Cooper Jr. hurt. Next three against soft NFC North pass defenses (GB, DET, CHI). This is the “others will not fight for him” WR.

### 10. Michael Mayer (TE, LV) — Priority 9 (TE-needy only)
**FAAB: 2–5%**

**Basis:** led Raiders in targets with Bowers out. Cap it hard — Bowers return rumors (even if optimistic) crush the ceiling. Streamer, not a season TE1 bid.

### 11. Rashod Bateman (WR, BAL) — Priority 10 / contingency stash
**FAAB: 1–3%**

**Basis:** Flowers is day-to-day (MRI); Lane fractured wrist. Bateman is the next man up in a Henry/Lamar run-first offense. Cheap insurance, not a must-start until Flowers’ status is bad.

### 12. Emmett Johnson (RB, KC) / Jonah Coleman (RB, DEN) — MNF holds
**FAAB: wait**

Do not bid until Arrowhead ends. Johnson is the reported Walker cuff; Coleman is the Payton committee dart. Revisit Tuesday morning.
