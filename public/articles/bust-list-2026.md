*By GH | thegavfather.com*

## The Pattern

Before the names — here is what the bust model 
actually looks for.

Players most likely to regress share these traits:

High touchdown rate relative to opportunity. 
Touchdowns are not repeatable. A player who scored 
12 TDs last year because his team kept feeding him 
near the goal line is not guaranteed to repeat that. 
The model discounts TD production heavily.

Garbage time inflation. The Gavfather System strips 
out all plays when teams are winning or losing by 
more than two scores. A receiver who padded his 
stats against prevent defenses in blowouts is not 
as good as his raw numbers suggest.

Outperformance vs opportunity. When a player's 
actual fantasy points significantly exceed what 
their target share and efficiency metrics project, 
regression is coming. The market bought the result. 
The model bought the process.

## The Bust List

**Patrick Mahomes, QB — Kansas City Chiefs**

The market has Mahomes as a top-3 QB. 
The Gavfather System has him QB9.

He tore his ACL and LCL in Week 15 of 2025. 
He is "hopeful" for Week 1. Hopeful is not 
confirmed. Hopeful is what you say when you 
genuinely do not know.

Even at full health: Eric Bieniemy is back as OC 
running a ground-and-pound scheme. More carries. 
Fewer attempts. Less opportunity.

The Star Loyalist will draft Mahomes in round 3 
convinced this is the year he bounces back.

This is not going to work out for them.

---

**Jalen Hurts, QB — Philadelphia Eagles**

Sean Mannion has never called a play at the NFL 
level. He was a backup quarterback. He was a QB 
coach. Now he is running the Eagles offense.

Hurts has never played for a first-time OC. 
The model applies a significant uncertainty penalty.

The market has him top-5 QB. The Gavfather has 
him QB6 with an injury risk flag.

Logan Roy would recognize this situation. 
A company being handed to someone who has never 
run one before. It might work. It usually doesn't.

---

**Dak Prescott, QB — Dallas Cowboys**

The market has Dak as QB5. 
The Gavfather has him QB20.

Fifteen spot positional disagreement.

Injury history. Dallas situation score of 49.7 
out of 100 — one of the lower scores among 
starting QB situations. A receiving corps that 
lost key pieces.

The market remembers the good Dak years. 
The model only knows what the numbers say now.

---

**Calvin Ridley, WR**

Our model has Ridley at WR184. 
The market has him at WR55.

That is a 129-spot positional disagreement.

He is 31. He has had multiple injury-interrupted 
seasons. His target share in 2025 did not justify 
his ADP. The age cliff for WRs is real.

The boom years are behind him. 
The market has not fully accepted this yet.

---

**The 49ers Stack**

Christian McCaffrey. George Kittle. Brock Purdy.

All three play TWO international games in 2026 — 
Melbourne in Week 1 and Mexico City in Week 11.

London games average 40.5 points. 
Germany games average 32 points. 
Melbourne is unknown but estimated similar.

Two weeks of suppressed scoring baked in before 
the season starts. Nobody is discounting 49ers 
players for this. The model flags all three.

Kittle especially. The market has him TE10. 
We have him TE2. But two international games 
means two weeks where his production is 
historically lower than normal.

Draft him with eyes open.
