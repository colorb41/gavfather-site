## The Biggest Winners

**Justin Herbert, QB — Los Angeles Chargers**

Mike McDaniel was an NFL head coach. 
He voluntarily stepped down to become 
Herbert's offensive coordinator.

That is not a lateral move. That is someone 
seeing something special and doing whatever 
it takes to be part of it.

McDaniel's scheme: motion heavy, quick release, 
designed to neutralize pass rush before it forms. 
Herbert called it "an oasis in the desert."

The Gavfather System applies plus 9 percent 
to Herbert's projection. The market has him 
QB11. We have him QB3. Eight spot positional 
gap. Largest QB disagreement between our 
model and consensus ADP right now.

---

**Zay Flowers, WR — Baltimore Ravens**

New Ravens OC Declan Doyle came from Ben 
Johnson's staff in Chicago where his offense 
ran the second highest play action rate in 
the NFL in 2025.

He plans to significantly increase Lamar 
Jackson's passing volume in 2026. Flowers 
is the primary target in that offense.

The Gavfather System applies plus 8 percent. 
The market has him as a mid-round WR3. 
We have him WR2 upside.

---

**Sam LaPorta, TE — Detroit Lions**

New Lions OC Drew Petzing made Trey McBride 
a star in Arizona with his TE-heavy scheme. 
LaPorta has a similar athletic profile and 
now plays in Detroit's elite offensive system.

The Gavfather System applies plus 9 percent 
for Petzing's TE usage tendencies. The market 
has LaPorta outside the top 5 at TE. We have 
him TE3.

---

**James Cook, RB — Buffalo Bills**

New Bills OC Pete Carmichael comes from the 
Sean Payton tree where the RB screen game 
has always been a valued piece of the offense.

Cook is the perfect fit for that role. 
The Gavfather System applies plus 4 percent. 
The market has him RB17. We have him RB6.

## The Biggest Concerns

**Jalen Hurts, QB — Philadelphia Eagles**

Sean Mannion has never called a play at the 
NFL level. He was a backup quarterback. 
He was a QB coach. Now he is running the 
Eagles offense.

Hurts has never played for a first-time OC. 
The Gavfather System applies minus 6 percent 
for the uncertainty. The market still has 
Hurts as a top 5 QB. We have him QB7 
with an injury risk flag.

---

**Brock Bowers, TE — Las Vegas Raiders**

Andrew Janocko has never been an offensive 
coordinator at any level of professional 
football. First time ever. Maximum uncertainty 
for every Raiders player.

The Gavfather System applies minus 7 percent. 
The market has Bowers as a top 3 TE. 
We have him TE4 with a significant 
uncertainty discount.

---

**Derrick Henry, RB — Baltimore Ravens**

The same Declan Doyle who helps Zay Flowers 
hurts Derrick Henry. More passing volume 
means fewer carries. The model applies 
minus 5 percent to Henry's projection.

He is 32. The age cliff penalty already applies. 
Now add the scheme change on top.

The market still reaches for Henry as a 
top 15 RB. The Gavfather has him RB9 
with both flags active.

## The Teams Nobody Is Talking About

**Cleveland Browns — Travis Switzer, new OC**

Switzer came from Baltimore where the Ravens 
led the NFL in rushing yards per game under 
his coordination. He brings that identity 
to Cleveland.

If the Browns have a competent RB — and 
with Darnold gone they are starting fresh 
at multiple positions — the run game volume 
will be there. Worth monitoring for late 
round RB value.

**Los Angeles Chargers — full system upgrade**

McDaniel does not just help Herbert. 
The entire offense runs differently. 
Omarion Hampton as a rookie RB in a 
Shanahan outside zone scheme is exactly 
the profile that produces early breakouts. 
He is a late round target with real upside.
