## Call 1: Justin Herbert is QB3.
### *Peter and MJ Were Always the Answer*

Peter Parker spends years not seeing what's 
right in front of him. MJ is there the whole 
time. Patient. Waiting for him to figure it out.

Justin Herbert is MJ.

The market has him at QB6 or QB7 — looking 
everywhere else while the answer stands 
right there.

Mike McDaniel was an NFL head coach. 
He voluntarily stepped down to become the 
Chargers offensive coordinator because he 
wanted to work with Herbert that badly.

Herbert called the new scheme 
"an oasis in the desert."

The Gavfather System applies +9% based on 
McDaniel's historical tendencies. Motion heavy. 
Quick release. Everything Herbert needed. 
Nothing he's had. Until now.

**The call:** Herbert QB3. Round 4.

He was always the answer. 
The market just hasn't figured it out yet.

## Call 2: Zay Flowers is a Top-10 Wide Receiver.
### *Harry Never Saw His Best Friend Standing There*

Harry Osborn spent years blaming Spider-Man

Zay Flowers is right there.

New Ravens OC Declan Doyle ran the second 
highest play action rate in the NFL last year.

Jackson's passing volume in 2026. Flowers 
is the primary target in that offense.

The model applies +8% for the new scheme.

The market has him as a mid-round WR3

**The call:** Flowers WR2 upside. 
Rounds 6-7.

Your best move has been standing next 
to you the whole time.

## Call 3: Patrick Mahomes is QB9.
### *Even Spider-Man Bleeds*

This is what makes Spider-Man different 
from every other superhero.

He's not invincible. He's a kid from Queens 
who hurts and bleeds and keeps going anyway. 
The emotional weight is real. The physical 
toll is real.

Mahomes tore his ACL AND his LCL in Week 15.

He's "hopeful" for Week 1. Hopeful is not 
confirmed. Hopeful is what you say when 
you genuinely don't know.

Even at full health: Bieniemy is back as OC 
running a ground-and-pound scheme. More carries. 
Fewer Mahomes attempts. Less opportunity.

The model applies -12% combined.

The market still has him top 3 QB because 
the market remembers who he was before 
he got hurt. The model only knows what 
the numbers say right now.

**The call:** Mahomes QB9.

Even the greatest ones bleed. 
Price accordingly.
