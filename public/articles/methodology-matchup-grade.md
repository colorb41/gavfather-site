# Why Matchup Grade Matters

Every PlayerCard on this site carries a matchup grade from 0–100. Here's what that number actually means — without the secret sauce.

## The short version

Higher is softer. A **70+** grade means the opponent's defensive profile has historically leaked fantasy points to that position. A sub-40 grade is a warning light.

## How to read it next to Outlook

- **SMASH + high matchup** = lean in
- **PLAY + average matchup** = start with confidence
- **NEUTRAL + elite matchup** = streaming candidate
- **FADE + any matchup** = sit unless you're desperate

## Tables you can trust

| Grade | Letter | Meaning |
|-------|--------|---------|
| 80–100 | A | Attack |
| 65–79 | B | Favorable |
| 50–64 | C | Neutral |
| 35–49 | D | Caution |
| 0–34 | F | Avoid |

The grade is one input. **Final Score** is the verdict. Use both.
