# The Moneyball Picks: Players the Market Is Getting Wrong

*By GH | thegavfather.com*

---

The entire premise of Moneyball is simple.

The market misevaluates players based on 
stats that look impressive but don't actually 
predict winning. Billy Beane found the stats 
that did predict winning and drafted the players 
who had them while everyone else was looking 
elsewhere.

The Gavfather System does the same thing 
with fantasy football.

22 years of play-by-play data. 175 engineered 
features. Machine learning models that don't 
care about highlight reels or podcast consensus.

Here are the players the market is getting wrong 
right now.

---

## The Method

The Gavfather System compares our positional 
rankings to consensus ADP from real fantasy 
drafts. When we rank a player significantly 
higher than the market � at their specific 
position � that is a BUY signal.

Not because we think we are smarter than 
everyone. Because the data says something 
the market hasn't priced in yet.

## The Sleeper List

**Justin Herbert, QB � Los Angeles Chargers**

Our rank: QB3. Market rank: QB11.

Eight spot positional disagreement. 
The largest QB gap between our model 
and consensus ADP right now.

Mike McDaniel left his head coaching job 
to become Herbert's offensive coordinator. 
Herbert called the new scheme 
"an oasis in the desert."

The model applies plus 9 percent for the 
scheme upgrade. The market hasn't moved.

Billy Beane would have Herbert on his team 
before anyone else noticed the price was wrong.

**Kyren Williams, RB � Los Angeles Rams**

Our rank: RB4. Market rank: RB16.

Twelve spot positional disagreement.

Williams is the bell cow back in a 
Sean McVay offense that historically 
commits to the run game. His carry share, 
snap share, and efficiency metrics are 
all elite. He posted RB1 numbers in 2025.

The market has him going in round 5-6. 
The model has him as a round 2-3 value.

**Courtland Sutton, WR � Denver Broncos**

Our rank: WR16. Market rank: WR30.

Fourteen spot positional disagreement.

Sutton is Bo Nix's primary target in a 
Sean Payton offense that acquired Jaylen 
Waddle to complement him. The Broncos 
passing volume is high. Sutton's route 
running and contested catch ability make 
him the ideal target in this system.

The market is sleeping on him. 
The model is not.

**DK Metcalf, WR � Seattle Seahawks**

Our rank: WR17. Market rank: WR33.

Sixteen spot positional disagreement.

Metcalf had a massive 2025 season as 
the clear WR1 in Seattle's offense. 
Jaxon Smith-Njigba gets more attention 
but Metcalf gets the contested targets 
in the red zone that turn into touchdowns.

The market discounts him. The model 
sees a proven WR1 with elite athleticism

**Brian Thomas Jr., WR � Jacksonville Jaguars**

Our rank: WR25. Market rank: WR37.

Twelve spot positional disagreement.

Thomas was one of the best rookie WRs 
in recent memory in 2025. With Trevor 
Lawrence healthy and targeting him 
as the clear WR1, the opportunity is 
there for a massive sophomore leap.

Note: Jacksonville plays two London games 
in 2026 (Weeks 6 and 7). International 
games historically score lower. 
Two weeks of suppressed output baked in.

Draft him knowing those two weeks exist.
The rest of the season he is a value.

Twenty-nine spot positional disagreement.

Jennings emerged as a legitimate WR1 
option in San Francisco in 2025 when 
healthy players were unavailable. 
The market treats him as a depth piece. 
The model sees a player with elite 
yards after contact and a role that

**Zach Charbonnet, RB**

Our rank: RB32. Market rank: RB44.

Twelve spot positional disagreement.

Charbonnet has shown he can handle a
